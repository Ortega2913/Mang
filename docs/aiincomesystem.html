03 — Week 1 Execution
Day-by-Day to $400
D01 Niche Lock + Product Concept + Hook Testing 3 hrs ▾
  • Pick your niche in 20 min. Choose ONE: (a) AI productivity for 9-to-5 workers, (b) ChatGPT prompt packs for small biz owners, (c) Notion templates for freelancers, (d) faceless YouTube automation guide. These have proven Gumroad buyer demand right now.
  • Research top 10 Gumroad products in your niche. Search "gumroad.com/discover" → filter by most popular. Screenshot the titles. These are your validation signals. Free
  • Draft 5 video hooks using ChatGPT. Prompt: "Write 5 TikTok hooks for a video about [niche topic] that create curiosity and fear of missing out. Under 8 words each." ChatGPT
  • Create 2 faceless videos using CapCut. Stock footage background + text overlay + trending audio. No face, no voice. Upload to TikTok AND Instagram Reels. CapCut
  • Set up your Gumroad account. Create a free store. Don't list anything yet — just get ready.
D02 Analyze + Build Your Core Product 3 hrs ▾
  • Check Day 1 video performance. If any video hit 500+ views, that hook is your winner. If not, try 3 new hooks tonight. Don't proceed to product creation until a hook sticks.
  • Build the product using AI. Prompt Claude/ChatGPT: "Create a complete [50 ChatGPT prompts for small business owners / Notion freelance client tracker / etc.] that I can sell as a PDF. Include instructions, use cases, and formatting." Claude
  • Format in Notion or Google Docs. Add a cover page, table of contents, section headers. Make it look designed — use a clean font, add some icons. Export as PDF. This takes 60 min max. Notion
  • Price it at $17. Not $5, not $50. $17 is the impulse-buy sweet spot with no perceived risk. You need 24 sales to hit $400.
  • Post 3 more videos. Keep the same hook format that worked. Volume is the algorithm signal.
D03 Launch Your Gumroad + Write Sales Copy 3 hrs ▾
  • Write your Gumroad product description with AI. Prompt: "Write a high-converting sales page for a digital product called [name]. It's for [audience]. It solves [problem]. Include a headline, 5 bullet benefits, a who-it's-for section, and a CTA. No fluff." Claude
  • Create a product thumbnail in Canva. Use a dark background, bold title text, and a mockup (Canva has free digital product mockups). This alone increases conversion by 30%+. Canva
  • List on Gumroad. Go live. Your link in bio on TikTok and Instagram now points here. This is the moment. Everything after this is traffic.
  • Set up Beehiiv free newsletter. Create a 1-page lead magnet (a mini version of your product or a bonus checklist). Anyone who buys gets invited to subscribe for more free content. Beehiiv
  • Post 3–5 videos with direct CTA. "Link in bio to get [benefit]." Make it specific. "Link in bio to get 50 ChatGPT prompts that saved me 3 hours today" beats "check my bio."
D04–05 Double Down on What's Working 3 hrs/day ▾
  • Identify your top video. The one with the most views/saves/shares. Remake it 3 different ways: different hook, different format, different audio. Replicate the signal.
  • Reply to EVERY comment. The algorithm rewards engagement. Ask commenters a question to extend the thread. This boosts organic reach for free.
  • Post your product link in Reddit. Find subreddits like r/ChatGPT, r/sidehustle, r/freelance. Write a genuinely helpful post, mention your product naturally in comments when relevant. Don't spam — add value first.
  • Create a "results" video. Screenshot your Gumroad dashboard (even 1–2 sales), post it. Social proof is a hook. "I made my first online sale with AI — here's exactly what I sold." This style consistently outperforms everything.
  • Target: 8–12 sales by end of Day 5. If you're under 5 sales, the issue is traffic volume — post more videos. If traffic is there but no sales, tweak the product title and thumbnail.
D06–07 Sprint to $400 + Build the Email Engine 3 hrs/day ▾
  • Create a second product at $27 — a "premium version" or companion pack. Existing buyers get it free as a loyalty reward (this earns trust + testimonials). New visitors see a bundle option.
  • Write your 3-email welcome sequence with AI. Email 1: deliver the freebie + personal story. Email 2: share your best free tip + soft mention of product. Email 3: limited-time offer on your $47 upsell (coming Week 2). This is where $11K/month comes from. Beehiiv
  • Cross-post to Pinterest. Pin your Canva graphics with keywords. Pinterest pins have a 3–6 month traffic tail. Free passive clicks starting in Week 3.
  • Sprint: post 5 videos on Day 7. Go hard. Use your best hooks again (re-post with slight variation). Volume on Day 7 closes the gap to $400.
  • Count your sales. If you're at $280–$350, email your list a flash offer: "48-hour discount — get both products for $27 total." That closes $400 in hours.

07 — The Kill List
What Will Actually Stop You
❌ Spending Week 1 building, not testing Fatal ▾

Post Day 1 before the product is done. Traffic data is more valuable than a polished product. A $17 PDF that 1,000 people see converts better than a $97 course 50 people see. Speed is your only advantage.

❌ Posting once and waiting Fatal ▾

The algorithm requires volume. 1 video every 2 days will never reach escape velocity. 3–5 videos per day for 7 days is the minimum viable signal. If you can only post 1/day, add 2 more platforms (YouTube Shorts + Pinterest).

❌ Pricing too low (<$15) or too high (>$35) in Week 1 Costly ▾

Under $12 and buyers perceive no value — paradoxically they buy less. Over $35 with no social proof and they need more convincing than a Gumroad page provides. $17 is optimal for zero-audience cold traffic.

❌ Skipping email capture Long-term killer ▾

Without an email list, you restart from zero every month. Every buyer who doesn't join your list is lost forever. The $11K/month number is built almost entirely on repeat buyers and email upsells. Set up Beehiiv on Day 1, even if it's ugly.
